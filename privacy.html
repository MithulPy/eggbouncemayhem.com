Egg Bounce Mayhem

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Egg Bounce Mayhem ("the game") is made by Mithul Koshy ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the game stores about you, why, who handles it, and how to delete it. It describes the game as of the date above.

What the game stores

  • A random player ID. When you first open the game it creates an anonymous account for you. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • Your game progress. Coins, gems, eggs, perks, level, missions, trophies, chests and daily rewards, so your progress is saved and checked for tampering.
  • Your scores and leaderboard name. Your best times, and the name shown next to them: a randomly generated name, or your Game Center nickname if you sign in to Game Center. The leaderboards show this name and your best times to other players.
  • Game Center ID (optional). If you choose to sign in to Game Center, its player ID is used to link your progress to your Apple account, so it comes back on a new phone. Game Center is optional; you can play fully without it.
  • Purchase records. When you buy gems, the game checks Apple's signed receipt and records the transaction ID, the pack, the time and your player ID, so that a purchase is not delivered twice.
  • Your time zone. Your phone's time-zone offset is sent with requests so the daily rewards and missions follow your own calendar.

The game does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising ID.

Who handles the data

  • Unity Technologies runs the game's online services (Unity Gaming Services: sign-in, the game server, cloud saves and leaderboards). As part of operating these services, Unity may derive an approximate (country-level) location from your internet connection and keep technical service logs. See Unity's privacy policy.
  • Apple handles Game Center sign-in and all payments. I never see your card or Apple account details. See Apple's privacy policy.

Your data is shared only with the service providers above, to run the game.

Why it's stored

  • To save and protect your progress and purchases.
  • To run the leaderboards and check that scores are genuine.
  • To deliver gems you buy, once, and prevent fraud.

How long it's kept

Your data is kept while you use the game. If you delete it (below), your save, scores and account are removed. Purchase records (transaction ID, pack, time, player ID) are kept after deletion, because they are needed to stop the same receipt being paid twice and for accounting.

Delete your data

In the game, open Settings > Delete my data. After two confirmation steps, the game deletes your save, your leaderboard scores and your account, and starts over as a new player. This can't be undone, and gems you bought are deleted too. Purchases are processed by Apple, and any refund request is decided by Apple under its own terms (reportaproblem.apple.com).

You can also ask me to delete your data, or ask what is stored about you, by email (below).

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It asks for no personal details from anyone.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to how it is used. You can make a request by email (below); requests are handled as applicable law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Law and Apple's terms

This policy is subject to the laws that apply where you live and to Apple's App Store terms. Nothing in it limits any rights you have under applicable law. Purchases are made through Apple and are subject to Apple's Media Services Terms and Conditions.
