Egg Bounce Mayhem

Support

Need help? Email mitikosstore@gmail.com. Include your leaderboard name (shown on the home screen) and what happened. I usually reply within a few days.

How do I play?

Tap to bounce your egg and keep it in the air. Dodge the kitchen chaos falling and flying around you, grab coins and golden yolks, and survive as long as you can. Spend coins and gems on new eggs and perks in the Shop.

I bought gems but didn't get them

Gems arrive once Apple confirms the purchase and the game's server checks it. If you were offline, they arrive the next time you open the game online. Still missing after that? Email me with the date and pack.

How do I get a refund?

All purchases are processed by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my progress on a new phone?

Sign in to Game Center in the game and your progress is linked to your Apple account, so it comes back when you install the game on a new device with the same Game Center account.

What are the chances in chests and the daily spin?

Every chance is shown in the game before you buy or spin: tap ODDS on a chest in the Shop, or SEE ODDS on the Daily Spin.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings > Delete my data in the game and confirm twice. Your save, scores and account are deleted. See the Privacy Policy for details.
